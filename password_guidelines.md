#### General Guidelines

Sharing credentials
- Don't share plain credentials via Slack or Email
- Use [Bitwarden Send](https://bitwarden.com/products/send/) or
- Encrypt your files. Share file + password on separate channels like Slack and Email

Staff & Apprentices
- Staff should use the Bitwarden vault to access shared team accounts.
- Apprentices should not share credentials with other apprentices.
- Staff will securely share credentials with apprentices

#### Bitwarden Send

[Bitwarden Send](https://bitwarden.com/products/send/) is a secure, end-to-end encrypted feature that lets you share sensitive text or files with anyone via a temporary, generated link. 
The recipient *does not* need a Bitwarden account to view or download the shared content.

Features

- **What you can share**
  - Free users can share **Text**, up to 1,000 characters.
  - Premium users can share **Text** and **Files**, up to 500 MB.
- **Options**
  - Expiration date, up to 30 days. Default is 7 days.
  - Optional password
  - View limit
  - One-time viewing via "Destruct-on-Open"
- **Privacy**
  - Hide your email address from the recipient
  - Hide text/passwords by default until clicked

Steps

1. Open Bitwarden (web, browser extension, desktop or mobile app).
2. Click on **Send** tab.
3. Click **New Send**.
4. Choose **Text** or **File**.
5. Add message or file, and give the Send a name.
6. Set your security options (expiration, view limits, password).
7. Click **Save** to get an encrypted link.
8. Copy and share it with your recipient.
